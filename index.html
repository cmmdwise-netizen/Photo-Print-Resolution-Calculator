Understanding Print Resolution (DPI & PPI)

What is 300 DPI? DPI stands for Dots Per Inch (often interchangeable with PPI or Pixels Per Inch for digital screens). 300 DPI is the universal gold standard for high-end professional photo printing, ensuring crisp details with no pixelation when held at normal viewing distance.

Inches to Pixels: If you are designing a photo album, poster, or print layout, knowing the exact pixel dimensions required guarantees your lab won't stretch or compress your images.

Resolution to Max Print: If you have an image file from your camera or phone, this calculation tells you the largest physical size you can print before quality degrades.
